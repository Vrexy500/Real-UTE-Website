Shipping & Delivery Policy

Fast, secure, and reliable gadget delivery across Enugu and nationwide in Nigeria.

Deliveries Within Enugu (Same-Day)

For all orders placed within Enugu metropolis, customers get their gadgets delivered same-day right to their doorstep, office, or direct pickup at our showroom located at Zain Plaza, No 59 Chime Avenue, New Haven, Enugu.

Instant Pickup Option: You can also walk directly into our store to inspect, test, and collect your device immediately.

Nationwide Waybill & Shipping (2–3 Days)

We ship gadgets securely to any state across Nigeria. For all waybills outside Enugu, delivery typically takes 2 to 3 days depending on your exact location.

  • Flexible Transport Options: To ensure your fragile tech items (laptops, phones, tablets, and screens) arrive safely and on time, we can dispatch your waybill either via flight or through express transport buses.
  • Tracking & Proof: Once your package is handed over to the logistics partner or airline cargo, we send you the waybill slip and driver/dispatch contact details via WhatsApp.

Packaging & Safety Assurance

Every gadget is double-checked and packaged securely with protective cushioning material before dispatch to guarantee it arrives in pristine, factory-sealed or tested condition.
